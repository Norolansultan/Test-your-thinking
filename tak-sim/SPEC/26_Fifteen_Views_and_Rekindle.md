## 2. Terms

- **Step:** a 20-minute world-state interval. Its number is the old cycle number (W1–W5, 1–20), as used in `18`, `19`, `24` and `03` §4.1 and §7. The world-state documents keep step numbers.
- **View:** what the participant experiences as a cycle, with one map state, one envelope and one decision phase. Session documents (`01`, `08`, `09`, `14`, `15`) use view numbers from this revision on.
- **A merged view** spans two steps. Its envelope projects to the end of the second step, and the validity label reads 40 minutes.

**Truth and model.** The fire model is unchanged: L3 was already the unsecured sector (`24`, the line-securing rule). Only unit positions and messages change. `tools/fire-ca/deployment.ts` `UNIT_PLAN` gets the two moves out at step 10 and the two moves back at step 13. The move build rule (450 m) is satisfied because both moves carry a reason.

**Why the sector leader (D5).** The participant cannot transmit, so the order cannot be theirs. An order from the sector leader keeps the participant's command authority intact and fits the autonomous-assets setting (`02` revision (o) §2), in which crews act within pre-agreed rules. The overruled warning then sits inside the participant's own organisation, where a commander should catch it.

**What the participant can do.** Find the objection (Haku, or a narrow question to Thinker), connect it with hinge rule 3, and allocate a unit back to L3 in V7 or V8. Allocations are recorded intentions. Scripted units do not follow them (identical stimulus), and they are scored against truth.

**Thinker (`14` revision (q)).** Nothing is special-cased. A broad question about the bog in V7–V8 ("onko suo hallinnassa?") fills the retrieved top-k with *in hand* status lines, so the objection is usually absent from the answer. A narrow question ("VPK suo", "kyteekö suo") retrieves the objection and the overruling reply. The live model may still mention a doubt. Exposure is logged and coded.

**The omission changes character.** It was *a possibility nobody represented*. It is now *a possibility someone raised and was overruled*: the envelope still does not represent it, but the record does. The cue set of `03` revision (g) (cycle-10 warm ground and peat smell, withdrawn) stays withdrawn. The objection is the one new cue. Anticipation of the rekindle is therefore easier than before, so **pilot 2 checks that V9 detection and anticipation stay within the 20–70% band** (`10`).

**Measures.**
- **Anticipation (new):** a unit allocated to L3, or Type E / SA content naming the bog, in V7–V8 before the rekindle.
- **Warning uptake (process):** whether and when the objection record was opened (Haku result opened, or cited in a Thinker answer the participant received).
- **H10 (secondary):** anticipation is less frequent in Haku + LLM than in Haku. The predicted mechanism is the broad-question drop. The test is two-sided.
