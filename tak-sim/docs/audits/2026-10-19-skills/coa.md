## Summary

1. The composed plan lets a commander *express* every wise/unwise × aligned/against intention, but the cell a plan lands in for the primary outcome (C vs B in V9, V10, V14) depends on which secondary-task label the participant picks for the same act on the rekindle ("Suora hyökkäys" = C; "Jälkisammutus ja varmistus" or "Linjan pito" = B). That is blocking (F1).
2. The keys for V9, V10, V12 and V13 hold up on Finnish doctrine (MV22 principles, KAL 4.4–4.6, 7.4). V11's "direct attack = unsound" rests on a forecast that is only on record, while the pushed drone report shows flames of 0.3–1 m, so the panel will probably key V11 as mixed (F5). V14 codes a main effort on the 0.3 ha flare-up as justified, which goes against the rescue-first principle and against SPEC/27 §7.4's own rule (F2, blocking).
3. In the premise table, V14 P2 "contradicted" conflicts with V13's own reasoning and with the V14 key, and it points the guide's flag at the wrong front (F3, blocking). P1 is too vague to be contradicted by a rekindle of the same fire, and the directive's stated crew-safety reason has no premise of its own (F4).
4. Labels: no "turvallisesti"-type tell words. The posture labels differ in length (2–5 words) and repeat the directive's own words. "Linjan pito" is ambiguous. Backfiring is not a plan option, and both burnout requests are cancelled (rare, OK). Freezes: V10 and V14 are fixed. V12's in-view freeze still cues the keyed sector (F10). V9 has no probe, and V13 has only the non-leading U item.
5. The panel drafts hold 34 plans (5–6 per view), written in Finnish with English keys, and include "probe" plans for the contested cells: `coa_option_key.csv` and `coa_rating_sheet.csv`.

## Conflicts with SPEC/30 needing a decision

1. **Grid per decision point vs cells across views.** The skill (step 3) wants every cell covered in each decision point. SPEC/16 y.4 and SPEC/27 §5.1 key no A in V9/V10/V14 and no C in V11–V13 by design. The drafts therefore include probes that could fill the "impossible" cell: V9-f, V10-f and V14-d (a possible A); V11-c, V11-f, V12-e and V13-e (a possible C). If the panel agrees on any of them, the view becomes *mixed* (SPEC/27 §3.5) and drops out of H13 or H14 under SPEC/30 §8's 4-of-5 rule, and the power in SPEC/30 §11 (three no-fit views) no longer holds. **Decision:** keep the probes (recommended: an unbiased key needs them) and pre-register the fallback (H13 on two views; H14 on two views).
2. **What the panel keys.** In SPEC/16 t.3 the panel *names the sound posture(s)* per view. The skill, and these drafts, have the panel *rate representative plans* 1–7, blind to strategy labels. The posture field cannot separate the secondary-task variants that decide C vs B (F1). **Decision:** derive the posture × sector × secondary-task key from plan ratings (recommended), or extend t.3 with an explicit rule for secondary tasks on the deciding front.
3. **Freeze and probe cueing.** The skill (step 1b) says freezes and decisions must not cue each other. SPEC/30 §5.1 keeps V12's in-view freeze and accepts that V10's post-plan freeze cues V11 (`front_r2_2h` points at R2). The V8 and V11 SPAM items and V13's U item also sit before plans. **Decision:** V12 moves after the plan, or `afterFreeze` is pre-registered as primary for V12 (F10, F11).
4. **Information rule.** The skill says no option may rely on facts the participant does not have. SPEC/27 §7.1 keeps deciding quantities recorded-only, so the keys rest on records that are reachable but not pushed: V9 (size), V10 (size), V11 (forecast) and V14 (size). A Haku participant who plans on pushed facts alone is coded against that key. **Decision:** confirm that the rule is "reachable by the view's end", and that the panel sees every reachable record (SPEC/16 t.3), not only the pushed ones.
5. **SPEC/30 §3 draft premise statuses.** V14 P2 "contradicted" (F3) and P1 at V9/V10/V14 (F4) need a panel and researcher decision before the table is hashed. Changing them, or the premises, is a treatment revision.
6. **Parallel and shuffled options vs a fixed, directive-mirroring form.** The skill wants parallel, shuffled options. SPEC/16 t.1 fixes the posture order (direct attack first) and, since round 2, has labels that mirror the directive. **Decision:** keep the fixed order (identical stimulus) or randomise per participant (logged); fix the label lengths (F9).
