# Radio and message-stream realism audit: tak-sim (head 7394df8)

Auditor: Phase-1, read-only. Skill: `wildfire-radio-realism` (references read: `finnish_radio_conventions.md`, `kalajoki_reference.md`, `mpv25_extract.md`, `fire_sa_evidence.md`, plus `sources.md`). SM 2021:35 and the VIRVE instruction are held at abstract level only, so every claim below about the national call-sign scheme or talk-group names is marked VERIFY.

## Summary

1. **Scope.** I read in full: all 69 radio items, all 32 story bubbles (`CYCLE_STORY`, 6 of them recorded), the designed events, the directive, the phase-A intent and the briefing texts. I also read all 154 pushed (T1) and all 84 lateral (T4) traffic records, all 98 V9/V10 records, the V14 pushed, lateral and sensor records, and all 48 records that a freeze or SPAM key cites. For the remaining 583 T2/T3 records I took a 1-in-10 systematic sample (59). The English was checked for all of these, and an automated number check ran on all 957 FI/EN pairs.
2. **No blocking defects.** I found no Kalajoki place names, call signs or dates, and no US ICS terms in the Finnish. The pushed stream is identical in both groups (`preview.ts:84`: `radio`→directed, Thinker cells→substitutive; the pushed route is the same in both). Every key record arrives no later than its item's view.
3. **Should-fix items:**
   - Call signs: EK and KY are probably the real Etelä-Karjala and Kymenlaakso codes, and the roster names those regions. OUT1/OUT2, SPOT and VOL1 are English placeholders, and VPK is not a call sign at all.
   - Generator artefacts: sentences duplicated inside one message, identical phrasing across units, and impossible values (4 % of a tank lasting 20 min, a lookout seeing the head at 3 000 m, a pushed advance of 51–68 m/min).
   - UGV1's status records contradict the pushed report that it is stuck.
   - Wrong or doubtful terms: *pistepalo*, *kyti*, *kylläinen*, *komentopaikka*, *teho*.
4. **Deciding facts.** V9, V10 and V14-P1 follow the rule: the existence is pushed and the size or rate is recorded. Three weak points:
   - In V14 (step 20), road-crew lines push the SE front's distance to R2 (EK11 600 m, KY21 550 m). SPEC/27 §7.2 says that distance stays recorded, and it is the key of the L3 freeze item `se_front_r2`.
   - In V10, "uusi rintama" (new front) is pushed and suggests a large fire.
   - The recorded-only SPOT second passes need an emergency-centre-log framing to read as realistic. The domain question stays open.
5. **English.** The numbers match in every pair. Four things to fix: "kaakkoissivusta" is left untranslated in 5 records; *pohjoisreuna* is rendered "north flank" in 100 records but "north edge" in the items; one translation names a keyed place (Lakeside Cottages); and the wind-direction wording is ambiguous.

## Conflicts with SPEC/30 needing a decision

1. **Parity has a new meaning.** The skill requires every fact to reach "Radio" and "AI" in the same cycle. Under SPEC/30 both groups get the identical pushed stream and the identical record corpus, so parity between the groups holds by construction (verified: `preview.ts:84`, and `demo-map.ts` / `traffic-atoms.ts` give pushed atoms the same route in the directed and substitutive conditions). The skill's channel parity now maps to pushed vs recorded, and SPEC/27 §7.1 makes that asymmetric on purpose (deciding quantities are recorded only). **Decision:** record that the skill's parity rule applies between groups, not between pushed and recorded. I did not run `parity_check.py`: it needs fact-id tagging the corpus does not have, and it compares two channels that no longer map to the two groups.
2. **The guide's premise line (SPEC/30 §3)** is extra synthesis for one group from V8 on. The skill allows that ("differences come only from the channel and synthesis"), and the line cites only records both groups can open. Its times match the records (P2 at 21 740 s is EK16's 19:22 radio; P1 at 21 780 s is SPOT's 19:23 bubble). **No conflict.**
3. **Recorded-only deciding quantities vs realism** (SPEC/27 §7.1, still open; DF-01 to DF-03). A real PTJ would hear, or ask for, a new fire's size within minutes, and participants cannot transmit (SPEC/02 (o)). Options:
   - (a) keep the design, and make the recorded quantities read as emergency-centre log entries or answers given on request ("kysyttäessä", as the T3 tier already does);
   - (b) push a qualitative size together with the existence, which weakens the no-fit manipulation;
   - (c) keep the design and say in the briefing that units' answers to the command post appear in the records.

   Needs the domain reviewer.
4. **V14's R2 distance** is pushed in step 20 (DF-03). This conflicts with SPEC/27 §7.2 and with SPEC/30 §5.1's rule that no key may be answerable by recall (`se_front_r2`). Decide whether to reword the two road-crew lines, or redesign the item.
5. **Real area codes and regions** (SPEC/18 §8.1 and the roster) vs the skill's invented-prefix rule (CS-01). Keep them (English majority, generated terrain), or rename them for the Finnish sessions (a corpus-wide edit and a Thinker re-index).
6. **Text-only delivery in both groups** meets skill step 4b (same modality). **No conflict.**
7. **Kalajoki dynamics are reused**: rekindle at the origin's edge after apparent control, a farmer's slurry tanker carrying water, a spotting-flight report, a late-July drought. The skill allows dynamics, and no names, dates or call signs are reused. The slurry tanker (evt.c20.020) is the most Kalajoki-specific surface detail: keep it or swap it.
